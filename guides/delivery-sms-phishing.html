Delivery & Parcel SMS Scams (Smishing): How to Spot Them

Fake delivery and customs-fee texts are the most common smishing lure. Learn the tells, check links safely and what to do if you paid.

Updated October 2026 ·

“Your parcel is on hold. Pay €1.99 to reschedule.” The small fee is the bait. The real target is your full card details and the one-time code your bank sends next.

Tells

  • You weren't expecting a parcel, or the text doesn't say which one
  • A shortened or odd-looking link (extra words, hyphens, unusual domain endings)
  • A small, oddly specific fee and a deadline
  • Sent from a regular mobile number, not the courier's registered sender name

Do this instead

Open the courier's app or type its website yourself and enter the tracking number. Never use the link in the text.

If you entered card details

Call your bank now, block the card, and watch for follow-up “bank security” calls. Those are often the same gang. Read the script.
