053 numbers in Türkiye: Turkcell's mobile range

In Türkiye, mobile numbers beginning 0530 to 0539 belong to the range originally allocated to Turkcell, the country's largest mobile operator. A Turkish mobile number has 10 digits after the trunk 0: 0 (53x) xxx xx xx, or +90 53x xxx xx xx internationally. Since mobile number portability arrived, a 053x number may now be served by Vodafone or Türk Telekom. The prefix tells you where the number started, not where it is today.

How to call 🇹🇷 053 from abroad

From abroad dial your exit code (00 in most countries, 011 from North America), then 90, then the number without the leading 0: 00 90 532 123 45 67. Inside Türkiye: 0532 123 45 67.

Common scam patterns using these numbers

  • Fake bank “security” calls asking for SMS one-time codes (OTP) to “cancel a suspicious transaction”
  • Impersonated police or prosecutors claiming your ID was used in a crime and asking you to move money to a “safe account”
  • Fake cargo/parcel SMS with payment links
  • Investment and crypto groups promoted by calls from 053x numbers using spoofed caller ID

Golden rule: no real bank, police force or government agency will ask for your one-time code, PIN, remote access, gift cards or crypto. Hang up and call back on a number you look up yourself.

Where to report

Hang up, call your bank on the number printed on your card, and contact the police (155) if money moved. Report spam SMS to your operator. Then report the number on 053053 so others are warned. Full list: official reporting by country.

FAQ

Is every 053 number in Türkiye a Turkcell number?

It started in Turkcell's range, but with number portability it may now be on another operator.

Why do I get calls from +90 53x when I don't know anyone in Türkiye?

It may be a legitimate business or someone you know, but unknown international mobile calls are commonly used for one-ring and investment scams. Don't call back unless you can verify who it is.

How many digits does a Turkish mobile number have?

10 digits after the 0 (11 with the 0), or +90 followed by 10 digits.
