053 in South Korea: Daegu area code

053 is the telephone area code of Daegu, South Korea's fourth-largest city (around 2.4 million people). Landlines look like 053-xxx-xxxx inside Korea and +82 53 xxx xxxx from abroad. Korean mobiles use 010.

How to call 🇰🇷 053 from abroad

From abroad: exit code + 82 + number without the 0: 00 82 53 123 4567. Inside Korea: 053-123-4567.

Common scam patterns using these numbers

  • “Voice phishing” (보이스피싱) from callers posing as prosecutors, police or financial regulators
  • Loan-offer scams that ask for “fees” or app installs
  • Family-in-trouble messages from new numbers (“Mum, my phone broke…”)
  • Spoofed 070 internet-phone numbers pretending to be local

Golden rule: no real bank, police force or government agency will ask for your one-time code, PIN, remote access, gift cards or crypto. Hang up and call back on a number you look up yourself.

Where to report

Call the police (112) if money moved. KISA handles spam and hacking reports on 118. Contact your bank immediately to freeze transfers. Then report the number on 053053 so others are warned. Full list: official reporting by country.

FAQ

Is 053 a mobile number in Korea?

No. 053 is the Daegu landline area code. Korean mobiles start with 010.

What time is it in Daegu?

Korea Standard Time (UTC+9), shown live on this page.

What are 070 numbers?

Internet-phone (VoIP) numbers. They are legitimate but easy to obtain, so scammers use them.
