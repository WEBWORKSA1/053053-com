UTC+05:30: the half-hour zone of 1.5 billion people

India Standard Time (IST) and Sri Lanka Standard Time (SLST) both run at UTC+05:30 all year, with no daylight saving. Read “053053” as a clock and you get 05:30 · 05:30: India's and Sri Lanka's offset, twice.

FAQ

Why is India on a half-hour time zone?

India adopted a single national time centred on the 82.5°E meridian, which is 5½ hours ahead of Greenwich. One clock for the whole country was a deliberate choice.

Does India use daylight saving time?

No. IST is UTC+05:30 all year.

Is Sri Lanka also UTC+05:30?

Yes. Sri Lanka Standard Time has been UTC+05:30 since 2006.

Which other places use odd offsets?

Nepal is UTC+05:45, Afghanistan UTC+04:30 and Iran UTC+03:30, plus several Australian and Pacific zones.
